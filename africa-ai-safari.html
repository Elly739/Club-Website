The Africa AI Safari

What if Africa does not just adopt AI, but defines how AI solves African problems?

By Okello EllyPublished 3 August 2026

Over the past few weeks, I have been immersed in two incredible learning experiences: the AI module in the McKinsey Forward Program and the AI Safari by Power Learn Project. One idea has continued to stay with me.

Build around the problems we choose to solve

The future of AI in Africa will not be determined by how many tools we use. It will be determined by the problems we choose to solve.

AI Safari challenged participants to think beyond prompting chatbots and to build AI systems around African realities, including:

  • Farmers operating with intermittent internet access.
  • Healthcare workers serving remote communities.
  • Entrepreneurs building businesses with limited resources.
  • Financial systems shaped by mobile money as well as traditional banking.
  • Solutions designed for local languages, cultures, and regulatory environments.

Useful technology starts with people

That shift in perspective matters. Conversations about AI often focus on the latest models or newest tools, but technology is only one part of the equation. The harder challenge is designing systems that are practical, ethical, accessible, and genuinely useful for the communities they serve.

As software engineers, innovators, researchers, and builders, our responsibility is becoming clearer. We are not simply learning how AI works. We are learning to combine engineering, human-centred design, systems thinking, and local context to solve problems that have existed for decades.

“In Africa, we don’t just write code. We heal systems.”

That line changed how I think about software engineering. Every application we build, workflow we automate, and AI agent we design should ultimately improve someone’s life, strengthen an organisation, or create opportunities that did not exist before.

An opportunity to shape what comes next

Africa’s greatest opportunity is not becoming the biggest consumer of AI. It is becoming the continent that demonstrates how AI can be applied responsibly, inclusively, and innovatively to solve real-world challenges at scale.

The future belongs to builders who understand both technology and the people it exists to serve.

Still learning. Still building. Still believing Africa has one of the greatest innovation stories yet to be written.
