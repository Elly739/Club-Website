Research and projects

Questions worth asking.
Ideas worth building.

Explore student-led ideas across security, the environment, campus life, transport, finance, geospatial technology, and more. The club designs its projects to align with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals and corporate social responsibility priorities.

SECURITY AND IDENTITY

BIT

A biometric identity management system concept for institutions and events.

Meet the team and see details
OCEAN CONSERVATION

BLUE

An ocean intelligence platform supporting sustainable fisheries and conservation.

Meet the team and see details
SUSTAINABILITY AND REWARDS

SmartWaste

A behavior-driven platform that rewards people for responsible waste disposal.

Meet the team and see details
FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGY

ChatPay

A social payments app concept for peer-to-peer transfers, gifting, and small transactions within chat and social platforms.

Supports digital commerce and financial inclusion.
TRANSPORT

Mobi

A proposed mobile platform to connect matatus, ride-hailing, campus shuttles, and other travel options. The supplied notes describe early development, with integrations and pilot work still pending.

Designed to make everyday journeys easier to plan.
MOBILE SERVICES

RevvWash

A mobile-first vehicle care service concept with location-based bookings, distance-based pricing, digital payments, and mobile wash teams.

Supports SDGs 8, 9, 11, and 12.
URBAN RESILIENCE

Sentient

A geo-resilience platform concept combining geospatial data and predictive modelling to help communities and planners understand urban risks.

Connects city planning, climate data, and public safety.
STUDENT INNOVATION

Umi

A digital planner and intellectual property management platform concept to help students organise work, document ideas, and manage their innovation process.

Combines planning, collaboration, and idea protection.
DIGITAL COMMUNITIES

Wecket

A social platform concept centred on interest-based communities, personalised content, real-time chat, and privacy-conscious interaction.

Aims to support more meaningful online engagement.
INNOVATION PORTFOLIO

Fourt

An innovation company and portfolio concept that brings together service ideas in agriculture, accessibility, security, water, civic technology, and more.

Connects multiple ideas under a shared structure.
ONLINE SAFETY

SentryHub

A digital safety platform concept focused on scam awareness, community reporting, and early warnings to help people respond to cybercrime.

Focuses on protecting vulnerable communities.
GEOSPATIAL INTELLIGENCE

MinerAI

A geospatial artificial intelligence platform concept for turning satellite data into models, datasets, and practical insights about the Earth.

Applies spatial data to real-world decisions.
INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The Thinking Game

A patent management system project designed to record, track, and manage ideas and patent applications securely and transparently.

Helps innovators document and protect their work.
