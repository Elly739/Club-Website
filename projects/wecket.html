PROJECT OVERVIEW

Wecket is an interest-based social platform designed to encourage authentic community engagement.

APPROACH AND FEATURES

The concept includes Nebular AI for content curation, real-time chat, and optional premium features. The supplied notes describe early frontend work and a prototype; backend, onboarding, and privacy work were still in progress when documented.

IMPACT AND DIRECTION

The project emphasises community-centred design, user privacy, digital literacy, and wellbeing.
