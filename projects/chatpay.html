ChatPay

ChatPay is a social payments app concept for peer-to-peer commerce and micro-payments.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

ChatPay is a social payments app concept for peer-to-peer commerce and micro-payments.

APPROACH AND FEATURES

The proposed app connects payment services with social platforms for transfers, gifting, and small transactions, with transaction recommendations and user analytics.

IMPACT AND DIRECTION

The notes connect ChatPay to SDG 8 and SDG 9 and state that a patent process is underway.
