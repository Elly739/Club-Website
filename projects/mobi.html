PROJECT OVERVIEW

Mobi proposes to connect matatus, ride-hailing, campus shuttles, and eco-friendly travel options for students and commuters.

APPROACH AND FEATURES

The project notes (dated 2025) say the concept and business plan were completed and frontend work began; backend, live tracking, API connections, operator onboarding, approvals, and pilot testing were pending. Suggested revenue includes ride fees, subscriptions, advertising, and partnerships.

IMPACT AND DIRECTION

The stated vision is a trusted, simple, reliable, and safe transport platform.
