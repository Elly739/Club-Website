PROJECT OVERVIEW

BLUE responds to declining fish stocks, pollution, and limited maritime monitoring affecting coastal communities.

APPROACH AND FEATURES

The platform combines IoT sensors, satellite imagery, and AI analytics to monitor water quality, biodiversity, and maritime activity, with dashboards, predictive models, and sustainability recommendations.

IMPACT AND DIRECTION

The project aligns with SDG 14 (Life Below Water) and SDG 13 (Climate Action).
