Identity, safety and smart systems

BIT

A student-led Kenyan innovation project exploring secure identity verification for institutions and events, alongside performance monitoring, smart infrastructure, and IoT devices.

Project status
Current stage not stated in the supplied profile.
SDG alignment
SDG 8SDG 11
Intended impact
Explore safer, more reliable identity checks and performance monitoring for institutions and events.
BACKGROUND AND CONTEXT

BIT was created to address identity verification challenges in institutions and events, as well as personal and institutional performance monitoring in Kenya. Manual identification can be vulnerable to fraud, human error, and delays. The project explores AI-supported facial recognition and fingerprint scanning to make authentication faster and more reliable for universities, corporate offices, and public gatherings.

Since its inception in June 2025, this student-led initiative has emphasized safety, efficiency, and technological advancement. Its multidisciplinary team also works on product design and development, including smart infrastructure and Internet of Things (IoT) devices intended to support Kenya’s technology and innovation landscape.

TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE AND FEATURES

The proposed system combines facial recognition using Python-based AI algorithms, fingerprint scanning, and QR code verification. The supplied project brief describes AES-256 encryption for data protection, cloud-based storage for scalability, and a real-time monitoring dashboard with alerts for unauthorized access.

Other planned capabilities include attendance tracking, analytics dashboards, and integration with existing human resources and event management systems.

OPERATING MODEL AND MARKET

BIT is designed around a business-to-business software-as-a-service (B2B SaaS) model, with subscription tiers and enterprise licensing. Its modular approach is intended to let smaller organizations use core verification features while larger institutions adopt enterprise analytics and multi-site integration. Potential users include universities, government offices, and event organizers.

SOCIETAL AND SDG IMPACT

The project aligns with United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 11, Sustainable Cities and Communities, through its focus on public safety. It also links to Goal 8, Decent Work and Economic Growth. The project brief projects more than 150 direct jobs in technical support and AI training, plus over 500 indirect jobs in implementation and partnerships.

PROJECTED IMPACT AND FUTURE GROWTH

The supplied brief projects first-year revenue of KES 9,750,000. Future plans include expansion across East Africa, mobile-based verification, predictive AI analytics for security, and NFC-enabled identification solutions.
