One platform, three groups

Make better disposal
worth repeating.

SmartWaste connects individuals, institutions, and collection partners through participation, reporting, and rewards.

FOR INDIVIDUALS

Turn disposal into progress.

Scan a bin's QR code to log disposal and earn points based on the waste category.

  • Eight categories, each with its own point value: plastic, paper, metal, glass, organic, e-waste, LED bulbs, and batteries
  • Redeem points for airtime, vouchers, and merchandise
  • Daily streaks, milestone achievements, and leaderboards by campus, estate, or community
  • Track estimated CO₂ impact
  • Progressive web app with offline support through a service worker
FOR INSTITUTIONS

See campus sustainability activity.

Universities, schools, hospitals, estates, and municipalities can review disposal patterns and participation.

  • Institution accounts are created by an administrator
  • Staff select their institution at sign-up and receive access after approval
  • Analytics for disposal trends, participation, and waste categories
  • Reports for CO₂ savings and waste diverted from landfill
  • Pilot (trial), Starter, Pro, and Enterprise subscription plans
FOR COLLECTION PARTNERS

Coordinate collection and recovery.

Collection organisations can apply for access and coordinate verified waste recovery.

  • Partners apply at sign-up with organisation details and await administrator approval
  • Manage bin collection schedules and requests
  • Record recovered quantities, weights, and recycled items
  • Track facilitation fees for verified waste stream coordination
